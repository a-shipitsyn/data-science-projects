# DX-10 door station — installation manual

## Overview
The DX-10 is a single-button video door station for private houses.
It has a 2 MP camera with a 110° viewing angle, infrared night illumination
and a built-in proximity card reader (125 kHz).

## Power supply
The DX-10 is powered from a 12 V DC supply, maximum current 400 mA.
Use the PS-12 power unit or any stabilised 12 V supply rated at least 1 A.
Do not power the door station from the lock circuit.

## Wiring
Connect the door station to the indoor monitor with a 4-wire cable:
audio, video, power and ground. The maximum cable length is 50 m for
0.5 mm² wires and 80 m for 0.75 mm² wires.

## Lock connection
The built-in relay switches an electromagnetic or electromechanical lock,
maximum 12 V, 1 A. The unlock time is adjustable from 1 to 9 seconds
(default 3 seconds) with the UNLOCK TIME trimmer on the back panel.

## Mounting
Mount the door station at a height of 1.5–1.6 m. Avoid direct sunlight on the
camera lens. Use the RF-1 rain cover for outdoor installation.
