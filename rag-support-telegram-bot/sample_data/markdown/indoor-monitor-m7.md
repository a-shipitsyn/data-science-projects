# M7 indoor monitor — user manual

## Overview
The M7 is a 7-inch colour indoor monitor with hands-free audio.
Up to two DX-10 door stations and two cameras can be connected.

## Power supply
The M7 is powered from 230 V AC mains through the supplied adapter
(output 15 V DC, 1 A). Power consumption is 6 W in standby.

## Memory
The M7 records a photo of every visitor when nobody answers the call.
Up to 100 photos are stored in internal memory; with a microSD card
(up to 32 GB) video clips of 10 seconds are recorded instead.

## Ringtones and volume
Twelve ringtones are available. Press MENU > Sound to change the ringtone.
The ring volume has 5 levels; the do-not-disturb mode mutes the ringtone for 8 hours.

## Reset to factory settings
Hold the MENU and UNLOCK buttons together for 10 seconds until the screen
flashes. All settings are reset; stored photos are kept.
